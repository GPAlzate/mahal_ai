# mahal ai

## Product Purpose
A mobile-first bill-splitting web app for Filipino friend groups. Users upload a receipt photo, AI parses the line items, participants are added, and items are assigned to each person. A shareable link lets anyone in the group see their split. "Mahal" means both "love" and "expensive" in Filipino — the name is a pun.

## Register
product

## Users
Filipino young adults (Gen Z / early millennial) splitting food bills at restaurants, convenience stores, or late-night food runs with friends. Casual, social context. Users share receipts via a short code. Some users are the "receipt owner" (uploader), others are "joiners" who arrive via share link.

## Brand & Tone
Playful, warm, direct. Neobrutalist aesthetic: cream background (#fff9ef), bold black borders, hard offset shadows, yellow (#FFD700) as the primary CTA color. DM Sans for headings, DM Mono for labels and data. The logo is "mahal ai <3" in a black rotated badge. Casual Filipino internet energy — not corporate, not sterile.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (no blue gradients, no purple AI aesthetic)
- Corporate fintech apps (no navy + gold, no "seamless" copy)
- Venmo/PayPal clones (no peer-to-peer payment UI clichés)

## Strategic Principles
- Mobile-first, PWA-ready
- Speed: receipt parsing starts the moment a photo is selected
- Minimal friction: guests can view splits without an account; sign-up is optional
- Conversion: signed-in users get receipt history; sign-up teasers use FOMO (blurred receipts)

## Key Screens
- Home: upload receipt / create manual receipt / my receipts (signed-in) / view shared receipt
- Participants: who's splitting the bill
- Assign: drag/tap to assign line items to participants
- Summary (/[code]): shareable receipt breakdown with per-person totals
- Settings: display name + GCash number
